# NeuroNest diagnostic-data decision

**26 October 2026 update:** a separate, non-clinical age-reference model now uses
public CC BY 4.0 SMOCC milestone data. Only aggregate parameters are bundled.
See [source, data-quality checks, evaluation and limits](milestone-reference.md).
This does not promote a diagnostic model or establish India/Malayalam validity.

**Decision date:** 8 September 2026
**Scope:** India, children and adolescents, English and Malayalam.

No real patient dataset is added to this repository. A public table with an
"autism" label is not enough to train a diagnostic product: the label may be
derived from the same questionnaire answers used as inputs, lack an independent
clinical reference standard, omit permission for this use, or be unrepresentative
of the intended population. The repository keeps only the deterministic synthetic
fixture generated by `services/diagnostic/pipeline.py synthetic-fixture`; it
exercises ingestion, grouped splits, evaluation, provenance and promotion refusal.
It cannot be promoted or used to make a clinical prediction.

## Validated-model integration review — 26 October 2026

**Outcome:** no candidate reviewed can produce validated individual disorder
probabilities from NeuroNest's current six age-banded milestone answers. No model,
questionnaire content, patient data transfer, readiness override or deployment is
added by this review. The live `/v1/readiness/` response was checked: diagnostic
prediction and clinical instruments remain unavailable; the assistant is ready.

| Candidate | Clinical evidence and required input | Integration finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas Dx / Cognoa ASD Diagnosis Aid | Prescription autism diagnostic aid for healthcare professionals, ages 18–72 months with developmental concerns. It uses its own caregiver questionnaire, patient video analysis and healthcare-provider questionnaire. Outputs are positive, negative or indeterminate for autism, not probabilities for multiple disorders. | The strongest clinical AI product found in this review, but not an inference substitute for our milestone checklist. Official material checked provides clinician onboarding, not a public integration contract or downloadable model. Vendor access and the complete supported clinical workflow are prerequisites. India availability and Malayalam support are unconfirmed. |
| AutMedAI | Peer-reviewed ASD prediction research with independent-cohort evaluation. Inputs are medical/background and developmental-history features from SPARK; these are different measurements from our six answers. | The authors' public repository contains development code. `autmedai.py` loads a separate `v8_data_comp_all.pkl` and trains XGBoost models; the repository inspected does not ship trained weights or that dataset. A research result does not supply a deployable, locally calibrated clinical predictor. |
| M-CHAT-R/F | Autism screening for ages 16–30 months using the complete 20-item questionnaire and the prescribed follow-up/scoring process. It is a screening instrument, not an ML probability model. | Feasible as a **separate** age-eligible screening workflow after electronic-use rights and the full faithful implementation are established. Existing milestone answers cannot replace its questions. The rights holder lists a Malayalam translation, but that alone does not establish calibrated individual probabilities. |
| SWYC | Broader developmental/behavioral screening using complete age-specific forms and scoring. | A possible broader screening workflow, not a multi-disorder prediction model. TEAM UP requests contact for electronic implementation and disallows modifications to the screening components. Our selected milestone questions are not an equivalent administration. |

Primary sources checked:

- Canvas Dx: [FDA original clinical review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN200069.pdf), [2025 clearance and intended use](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf24/K243558.pdf), [official practice onboarding](https://cognoa.com/get-demo/). The original trial was conducted at US sites; its evidence cannot be represented as a NeuroNest Malayalam validation.
- AutMedAI: [2024 JAMA Network Open study](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2822394), [authors' development code](https://github.com/Tammimies-Lab/ASD_Prediction_ML_Rajagopalan/blob/main/autmedai.py). No downloaded research code was executed and no pickle was loaded.
- M-CHAT-R/F: [electronic-use permissions](https://www.mchatscreen.com/mchat-rf/), [official translations](https://www.mchatscreen.com/mchat-rf/translations/), [Malayalam study](https://cdckerala.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/1753693526185258479.pdf). That study explicitly reports that criterion validity could not be evaluated; do not present it as local probability calibration.
- SWYC: [steward's implementation and modification guidance](https://www.teamupcenter.org/about-swyc/swyc-frequently-asked-questions/).

### Concrete connection requirements

For an actual clinical AI product, obtain vendor-supported integration access and
documentation, confirm intended population/languages and local availability with
the provider, and collect its required inputs in a separate consented workflow.
Then connect through the server, enforce child ownership and eligibility, and
verify completed, indeterminate, rejected and failed requests plus report
persistence. Preserve the provider's result meaning; do not turn a classification
or study accuracy into an individual percentage or a claim of "normal" development.

For the more accessible questionnaire route, obtain the applicable electronic-use
rights and reviewed English/Malayalam content, implement and verify the complete
instrument including follow-up, and report its actual screening category. The
existing diagnostic service is a gated integration boundary, not proof that a
clinical instrument is implemented or licensed. Do not enable it by inventing
approval receipts. Neither route can be completed by scraping additional milestone
text or supplying missing clinical answers with an LLM.

## Assistant evaluation data (not clinical-model training)

These sources may help test retrieval, translation and safe assistant behavior.
They do not establish autism screening validity, and none is added to a patient
record, a fine-tuning corpus or the knowledge collection without a separate
rights and clinical-content review.

The next safe asset is an original, consent-free evaluation set of 180–250
English, Malayalam and code-mixed prompts. Every row should have an expected
behavior, escalation condition, prohibited claim, cited source ID, native
Malayalam review and clinician review. It is an evaluation set, not a training
set. No simulated patient conversation may be represented as clinical evidence.

### Fine-tuning decision

No Unsloth run or external fine-tuning job is started for this release. A LoRA
or managed fine-tune can improve tone, language switching and routing only after
the team owns a de-identified, rights-cleared, clinician-reviewed corpus and a
held-out bilingual evaluation has passed. It cannot make an autism prediction
clinically valid. The assistant must continue to retrieve reviewed sources,
cite them, abstain where evidence is missing and escalate urgent concerns.

## Required next dataset

The clinically relevant asset is a prospective, permissioned partner-clinic
cohort with independently established clinical outcomes. Its protocol must define
the intended age range, recruitment sites, independent reference standard,
English/Malayalam review, consent and withdrawal processing, permitted model use,
and a held-out site/time evaluation. Split by family and site before preprocessing.

Until that work is complete, the diagnostic engine must return its explicit
unavailable/abstention response. This is safer and more useful than inventing a
clinical dataset from public questionnaires or internet images.

## Public-data recheck — 27 October 2026

Two closer public candidates were checked against the deployed milestone inputs:

- [D-score childdevdata](https://github.com/d-score/childdevdata/) provides
  CC BY 4.0 child-level age and developmental-item observations across multiple
  cohorts (28,465 records in the documented combined table). It is useful for
  developmental measurement research, but it has no disorder diagnosis target
  and its harmonized items are not NeuroNest's six CDC-derived prompts. It cannot
  train individual disorder probabilities.
- [Polish Q-CHAT dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/tmpkt2mfkg/2) has
  252 toddlers aged 18–24 months, Q-CHAT item scores, and ASD/typically-
  developing clinical groups. A later cross-cultural study reports testing
  [Q-CHAT models against a Polish clinical cohort](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-26131-9),
  but those Q-CHAT answers are not the NeuroNest milestone checklist. The
  [Autism Research Centre's terms](https://www.autismresearchcentre.com/research/tests/)
  also limit downloaded tests to research/non-commercial use and prohibit
  adaptation without permission.

Neither dataset is connected to `/predict`. Replacing a six-item milestone
response vector with Q-CHAT inputs, or treating age-norm milestone records as
clinical diagnoses, would create an unsupported model. The existing model gate
and explicit abstention remain in place until a compatible rights-cleared
instrument or a consented partner-clinic dataset is available.
